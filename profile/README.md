## What we do

Harbor Native is a fiat-to-crypto on-ramping (and off-ramping) protocol that lets people and businesses move value between local payment rails and blockchains — starting with **Stellar**, and expanding to other chains over time.

We handle the hard parts of on-ramping so wallets, apps, and businesses don't have to:

- 🏦 **Fiat rails** — local payment methods, bank transfers, and mobile money integrated behind a single API
- 🔁 **On-ramp & off-ramp** — fiat → stablecoins/assets and back, with predictable settlement
- ⚓ **Stellar-native** — built to take advantage of Stellar's speed, low fees, and native asset/anchor model
- 🌐 **Multi-chain roadmap** — expanding beyond Stellar so partners can on-ramp users into the ecosystems they actually build on
- 🔐 **Compliance-aware by design** — KYC/AML, transaction monitoring, and licensing considerations built into the flow, not bolted on after

## Why Stellar first

Stellar was purpose-built for payments and asset issuance — fast finality, sub-cent fees, and a mature anchor/SEP ecosystem for connecting fiat to onchain rails. That makes it a strong foundation for on-ramping infrastructure that needs to be cheap, fast, and reliable at scale, before we extend the same experience to other chains.
